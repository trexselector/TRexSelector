# Team T-Rex

The software centered around the Terminating-Random Experiments (T-Rex) selector, the TRexSelector C++
library and its R and Python packages, is developed by Team T-Rex. The team currently works at the
Technische Universität Darmstadt and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. The copyright
notices of the library and the packages name the team, and so do the package entries of the citations.

## Roles by package

The roles differ between the three packages.

- **R package TRexSelector.** Jasin Machkour is the package and IP owner, he proposed version 1.0.0 of
  the package. Fabian Scheidt is the main contributor, and further contributors may join. Simon Tien
  contributed contents to the R releases prior to 1.0.0 and is credited as a contributor in DESCRIPTION.
  Daniel P. Palomar and Michael Muma are IP owners.
- **Python package trexselector.** Arnau Vilella Pique and Fabian Scheidt are the owners. Arnau Vilella
  Pique proposed the 0.x versions of the package. From version 1.0.0 on, Fabian Scheidt is the main
  contributor and owner. Daniel P. Palomar and Michael Muma are IP owners.
- **C++ library TRexSelector.** Fabian Scheidt is the IP owner, the package owner and the sole
  contributor so far. Daniel P. Palomar and Michael Muma are IP owners.
